Privacy Policy for Line Animator

Last Updated: October 20, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Line Animator, developed by Zix, handles user information, including information processed through third-party services used for advertising, subscriptions, and app functionality.

By using Line Animator, you acknowledge the data practices described in this Privacy Policy.

1. Information We Collect

Line Animator does not require users to create an account or provide personal information such as:

  • Name
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • Physical address

The developer does not directly collect or upload user-created animation projects to external servers.

However, third-party services integrated into the app may automatically collect or process certain information, including:

  • Device information and identifiers
  • Advertising identifiers
  • App interactions and usage information
  • Diagnostic and performance information
  • Purchase and subscription information

The information processed depends on the third-party services used, device settings, and applicable privacy choices.

2. Permissions Used

Storage and Media Access

Line Animator may request storage or media access permissions depending on the Android version.

These permissions are used to:

  • Create and save animation projects
  • Import images selected by the user
  • Export animations as images or videos
  • Access user-selected files

Animation projects are stored locally on the user's device. The app does not intentionally access unrelated personal files.

Notification Permission

Line Animator may request notification permission to display notifications related to app operations, such as export progress or completion.

Users can manage notification permissions through their device settings.

Internet Access

Internet access may be used for:

  • Loading advertisements
  • Processing subscription purchases
  • Verifying subscription status
  • Accessing Google Play services

3. Third-Party Services

Line Animator integrates services provided by Google to support advertising, subscriptions, and other app functionality.

These services may include:

  • Google AdMob
  • Google Play Billing
  • Google Play Services
  • Google Play In-App Review

These services may process device information, app interactions, diagnostic data, and other information necessary to provide their functionality.

For more information, please review:

Google Privacy Policy

4. Advertising

Line Animator displays advertisements through Google AdMob.

Advertisements may include banner advertisements, interstitial advertisements, and rewarded advertisements.

Google AdMob and its advertising partners may collect or process information such as:

  • Advertising identifiers
  • Device information
  • IP address
  • Ad interactions
  • Approximate location inferred from network information

This information may be used for advertising delivery, measurement, fraud prevention, and, where permitted, advertising personalization.

Users may manage available advertising preferences through their Google account or device settings.

Users with an active premium subscription may receive an advertisement-free experience, depending on their subscription benefits.

For more information:

Google Advertising Privacy Information

5. Subscriptions and Payments

Line Animator offers optional premium subscriptions that provide access to additional features and benefits.

Available subscription plans may include:

  • Monthly subscription
  • Yearly subscription

All subscription purchases are processed securely through Google Play Billing.

The developer does not directly collect or store credit card numbers, debit card numbers, or other payment card details.

Google Play may process payment-related information according to its own privacy policies.

Line Animator may access limited purchase information, such as:

  • Subscription purchase status
  • Purchased subscription plan
  • Purchase identifiers or tokens
  • Subscription expiration or renewal status

This information is used to verify purchases, manage premium access, and restore subscription benefits.

Automatic Renewal

Monthly and yearly subscriptions automatically renew unless canceled before the next billing date, subject to Google Play's subscription terms.

Subscription prices and billing periods are displayed before purchase.

Subscription Cancellation

Users can manage or cancel subscriptions through Google Play.

Canceling a subscription generally prevents future renewals while allowing access to subscription benefits until the end of the current paid billing period.

Manage Google Play Subscriptions

Refunds

Refund requests are subject to applicable Google Play refund policies and consumer protection laws.

Google Play Refund Information

6. Data Storage

Animation projects, drawings, frames, layers, and exported files are stored locally on the user's device.

Line Animator does not automatically upload user-created animation projects to developer-operated servers.

Users are responsible for managing their locally stored projects and exported files.

Removing the app may delete app-specific project data, depending on Android storage and backup behavior.

7. In-App Reviews

Line Animator may occasionally request users to rate or review the app through the Google Play In-App Review service.

Reviews and ratings are handled by Google Play.

The developer does not directly collect personal information through the in-app review interface.

8. Data Security

Line Animator takes reasonable measures to protect locally stored application data.

Payments and subscription transactions are processed through Google Play.

However, no method of electronic storage or data transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

9. Children's Privacy

Line Animator is not specifically directed toward children under the age of 13.

The developer does not knowingly collect personal information directly from children.

If you believe that personal information relating to a child has been collected inappropriately, please contact us.

10. Your Privacy Choices

Users may:

  • Manage app permissions through Android settings
  • Manage advertising preferences through Google settings
  • Cancel subscriptions through Google Play
  • Delete locally stored animation projects
  • Uninstall the app to remove applicable app-specific data

For questions about privacy or data handling, users may contact the developer.

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated to reflect changes in app functionality, third-party services, or legal requirements.

Any updates will be published on this page with a revised "Last Updated" date.
